A note on scope

Read for language, not diagnosis.

WRH is non-clinical education. It is intended to build pattern literacy and practical language, not to assess, treat, or replace licensed care.

Facilitators: review local crisis contacts, stop criteria, and referral pathways before delivery. See the safety documentation.
