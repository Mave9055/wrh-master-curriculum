# WHEN SURVIVAL BEHAVIOR GETS MISREAD

### 1. RECOGNIZE

**Have you ever...**

been told you were being disrespectful when you thought you were protecting yourself?

Someone asks you a question.

You don't answer right away.

Maybe you're trying to figure out what to say.

Maybe you're watching the room.

Maybe you don't trust where the conversation is going.

The other person sees your silence.

They say:

> "Why are you ignoring me?"

Now you're irritated.

You think:

> *"I'm not ignoring you."*

But they already decided what your silence meant.

Now you're both reacting to something that started with a misunderstanding.

Or maybe you leave a conversation because you know you're getting too angry.

From your side:

> *"I'm trying not to make this worse."*

From their side:

> *"He's refusing to cooperate."*

Same behavior.

Two completely different stories.

---

### 2. NAME

This is:

## **Being misread.**

Someone sees what you do.

They don't automatically know **why** you did it.

So they fill in the missing information.

Sometimes they get it right.

Sometimes they don't.

---

### 3. EXPLAIN

People judge what they can see.

They can see you walk away.

They can see you stop talking.

They can see you miss an appointment.

They can see you get angry.

They usually cannot see everything happening inside you at that moment.

So they may create their own explanation.

And you may do the exact same thing to them.

You see their tone.

Their face.

Their decision.

Then your mind fills in the rest.

That's how two people can become certain they understand each other while both are wrong.

---

### 4. SEPARATE

Being misunderstood does not mean you have no responsibility.

You still control how you communicate.

And other people still have responsibility for how they treat you.

The goal isn't:

> **"Everyone needs to understand me."**

The goal is:

> **"I'll make my meaning as clear as I can."**

You can't control somebody else's interpretation.

You can control what you give them to work with.

---

### 5. INTERRUPT

When you think someone has misunderstood you, **say what you mean in one sentence.**

Try:

> **"I'm not refusing. I need a minute to think."**

Or:

> **"I'm leaving because I'm getting angry. I'll come back to this."**

Or:

> **"I missed it. Tell me what I need to do next."**

Short.

Clear.

No speech required.

---

### 6. PRACTICE

Imagine someone says:

> **"You're not listening."**

Your first reaction is:

*"Yes, I fucking am."*

Instead of arguing about whether they are right, practice giving them useful information.

Say:

> **"I am listening. Give me a second to answer."**

Now imagine they still don't understand.

That's okay.

You communicated clearly.

You don't have to win the argument about what they think of you.

You only have to make your position clear and choose what you do next.

---

### 7. CARRY IT

> **People can see your behavior; they cannot automatically see your reason.**
