Safety notice

This site is not crisis care.

WRH is not currently delivered through this website. It does not provide assessment, treatment, crisis intervention, medical care, or emergency instructions.

If someone may be in immediate danger

Call local emergency services. In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For non-immediate concerns, contact a qualified local health, behavioral-health, legal, or social-service professional as appropriate.

Future delivery requirement

No future WRH site may begin delivery without its own written emergency contacts, escalation responsibilities, referral pathways, confidentiality and record-handling rules, trained personnel, supervision access, and applicable legal review.
