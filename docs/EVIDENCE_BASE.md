# WRH Evidence Status

## Current status

WRH is in development. The material does **not** currently have a completed, independently reviewed claim-level evidence register, clinical endorsement, validated outcome study, or authorization for delivery.

No public-facing document should describe WRH as evidence-based, evidence-informed, clinically validated, or approved until those statements are supported by a published record.

## Required evidence record before release

For each material claim, the release record must identify:

1. The exact claim and the module/session where it appears.
2. The supporting primary or authoritative source, publication date, and link or identifier.
3. The applicable population and setting.
4. Strengths, limitations, and uncertainty.
5. Whether the claim is educational, clinical, legal, operational, or marketing language.
6. Reviewer name, relevant qualification, review date, decision, and required edits.
7. The revision version that implemented the decision.

## Scope boundary

Research references cannot substitute for clinical, legal/privacy, accessibility, or site-specific implementation review. This document is a release-control placeholder, not an evidence register.
