Release gate

A controlled pilot may be considered only after independent clinical and legal/privacy review, a real evidence register, approved site-specific safety procedures, qualified and supervised facilitators, a participant-records plan, accessibility review, and a documented release decision.

No organization should treat this website as an offer of certification, training, clinical support, crisis management, outcomes measurement, or implementation services.
