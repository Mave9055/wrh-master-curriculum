The operating frame

Education with a clear boundary.

WRH gives participants and facilitators a shared language for patterns that are often misread as defiance, weakness, noncompliance, or moral failure.

Non-clinical by design

WRH is psychoeducational. It is not therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or a replacement for licensed clinical care.

No-disclosure learning

Participants can learn the pattern without reliving or disclosing personal trauma history. The curriculum starts with observable mechanisms.

Safety-aware delivery

Facilitator stop criteria, referral pathways, crisis escalation expectations, and debrief practices are part of the implementation model.

Built for measurement

Pilots can track attendance, reflection, facilitator observations, and pre/post awareness without overstating what education can do.

Scope boundary

Pattern literacy is not a promise of treatment.

WRH is designed to support language-building, practical reflection, facilitator consistency, and implementation clarity. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace licensed care.

If someone is in immediate danger or crisis:
Pause the curriculum and use the local emergency or clinical support pathway defined by the program site.
