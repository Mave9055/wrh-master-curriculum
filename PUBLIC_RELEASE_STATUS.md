# WRH Public Release Status

**Status:** Development-stage informational site  
**Effective:** September 4, 2026  
**Delivery authorization:** None

The former public session catalog, pilot positioning, facilitator certification positioning, clinical/crisis material, and agency-specific operational references are withdrawn from public delivery pending controlled remediation.

## Public limitations

No public visitor, facilitator, organization, or institution is authorized by this repository or site to:

- deliver or adapt WRH;
- treat this material as a crisis protocol, clinical intervention, screening tool, or safety plan;
- represent WRH as evidence-based, clinically validated, reviewed, approved, certified, funded, contracted, or endorsed;
- collect participant records or use materials in a mandated setting.

## Minimum release gates

1. Claim-level evidence register with source and reviewer documentation.
2. Independent clinical, legal/privacy, accessibility, and site-implementation review.
3. Site-specific safety, escalation, confidentiality, records, and referral procedures.
4. Defined facilitator authorization, training, supervision, and fidelity controls.
5. Audience-separated participant, facilitator, internal, and revision documents.
6. Version manifest and documented release decision.

Archived source material remains preserved for remediation and must not be used as a delivery package.
